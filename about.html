About DevTools Hub

DevTools Hub is a free collection of everyday tools built for developers, testers, students and anyone who works with code. Our goal is simple: provide fast, reliable, no-sign-up tools that get the job done in one click.

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device, nothing is uploaded to a server, and no account is required. This makes our tools private, secure and always available.

Why we built this site

Developers waste a lot of time searching for tools that are slow, cluttered with ads, or require sign-up before use. We wanted a clean alternative: a focused set of utilities that load fast, work offline-friendly, and respect your privacy.

The site is maintained as a lightweight static project, which keeps pages fast and lets us keep every tool free for everyone.

What we offer

  • JSON Formatter & Validator - format, minify and validate JSON
  • Unix Timestamp Converter - convert between timestamps and readable dates
  • Base64 Encoder / Decoder - encode and decode text instantly
  • URL Encoder / Decoder - percent-encode and decode URLs
  • UUID Generator - generate version 4 UUIDs in bulk
  • Hash Generator - compute MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-512 hashes
  • Regex Tester - test regular expressions with live match highlighting

We plan to keep expanding this list with more utilities that developers ask for.
